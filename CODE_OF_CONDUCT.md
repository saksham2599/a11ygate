# Community code of conduct

A11yGate aims to be a respectful place for developers and people with different
access needs, backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.

- Discuss the work and its evidence; do not attack the person doing it.
- Welcome questions and corrections. Explain unfamiliar testing concepts clearly.
- Respect lived accessibility experience without treating one person's experience
  as representative of every user.
- Do not demand disclosure of disability, medical information, identity, or private
  circumstances to participate.
- Do not post harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or another person's
  private information.
- Ask before publishing someone else's feedback, recordings, or attribution.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and limit participation to
protect the community. When reporting conduct concerns, contact a maintainer
through an existing private channel. If none is available, request a private
contact route without publishing sensitive details. Repository issues are visible
to all collaborators while private and to everyone once public.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project
spaces managed by the maintainers.
